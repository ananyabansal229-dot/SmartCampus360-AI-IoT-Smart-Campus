
# Risks, Limitations, and Mitigation

## 1. Inaccurate Campus Information

**Risk:** Incorrect locations, menus, or facility information could mislead users.

**Mitigation:** Use institution-verified records, show update dates, and require authorised review.

## 2. Sensor Unavailability

**Risk:** Sensors may be offline or unavailable during early development.

**Mitigation:** Use clearly labelled sample data for demonstrations and add real sensor integration in later phases.

## 3. Incorrect AI Predictions

**Risk:** Predictions or anomaly alerts may be inaccurate.

**Mitigation:** Validate models against reliable data and require human review before important decisions.

## 4. Privacy and Access Control

**Risk:** Staff contact details and student complaints may contain sensitive information.

**Mitigation:** Collect only necessary information, restrict access by role, and avoid publishing personal data.

## 5. Campus Map Accuracy

**Risk:** Missing or incorrect routes could cause confusion.

**Mitigation:** Verify maps with the institution and test route accessibility before deployment.

## 6. Current Prototype Limitations

The current prototype uses illustrative campus readings and sample feature descriptions. It does not yet implement production authentication, real IoT monitoring, trained AI/ML prediction, or live navigation.

## Overall Approach

Develop the system in modules, validate data, test each feature, and obtain institutional approval before deployment.
